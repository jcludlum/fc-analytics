# Paillard of Flank Steak

A paillard is a thin slice of meat that's pounded and then grilled. This is a great dish when you need something quick but satisfying. We all have to rush home occasionally to make a spectacular dinner for two or more. The flank steak and shallot sauce take only a few minutes to prepare and cook. We serve it with crispy french fries. To drink with this meal, we'd choose a big wine that would hold up well with the sauce and french fries, such as a peppery zinfandel. Serves four.

## Ingredients

- 2 lb. flank steak, trimmed
- Olive oil
- 4 Tbs. butter
- 1 oz. shallots, sliced
- 1 1/2 tsp. parsley, chopped
- 1 Tbs. lemon juice

*** = component recipe

## Directions

1. To prepare the steak, hold your knife at a low angle, about 20°, and slice the flank steak across the grain into pieces about 1/4 in. thick. You will end up with slices the width of your steak and about 4 1/2 in. long.
2. Pound them with a mallet until they're 1/8 in. thick. Brush with olive oil.
3. To make the sauce, melt the butter in a frying pan. Add the shallots and saute until golden brown.
4. Add the parsley and lemon juice, stir, and then remove from heat.
5. Grill the paillards for 30 seconds each side.
6. If serving with french fries, arrange them on each plate first. Then place the paillards, season them with salt and pepper, and pour the sauce over the steak.
