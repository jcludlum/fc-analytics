# Ginger Marinade

Ginger marinade, made with ginger dice, a little white wine, and soy sauce, is great for fish or meat. Good proportions are one-quarter cup diced ginger and two tablespoons wine to each cup of soy.

## Ingredients

- 1/4 cup diced ginger
- 2 Tbs. white wine
- 1 cup soy sauce

*** = component recipe

## Directions

1. Combine the diced ginger, white wine, and soy sauce.
2. Use the mixture to marinate fish or meat.
3. When ready to cook, pour off the marinade, but leave the ginger dice on the food, allowing the flavor of the ginger to penetrate the food further while cooking.
