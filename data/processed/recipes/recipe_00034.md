# Basic Vinaigrette & Variations

A vinaigrette is an emulsion made in its simplest form from oil, vinegar, salt, and pepper. A little Dijon mustard is often included as well. The ratio of vinegar to oil is generally one part vinegar to three parts oil. Makes 1 cup.

## Ingredients

- 1/4 cup red- or white-wine vinegar
- 1 Tbs. Dijon mustard
- 1/4 tsp. salt, or to taste
- 1/8 tsp. pepper, or to taste
- 3/4 cup olive oil, vegetable oil or a combination

*** = component recipe

## Directions

1. In a small bowl, whisk together the vinegar, mustard, salt, and pepper.
2. Whisking constantly, add the oil in a slow, steady stream until completely incorporated. Place the bowl on a potholder or folded dishtowel to keep the bowl from spinning while you whisk.
3. Champagne and black peppercorn variation: Substitute two tablespoons champagne vinegar for the red-wine vinegar, and two tablespoons mayonnaise for the Dijon mustard. Use two teaspoons crushed whole black peppercorns in place of the pepper (to crush peppercorns, place them on a cutting board and press down on them with the bottom of a small, heavy saucepan until they're broken into small pieces). Add a teaspoon of minced shallots.
4. Lemon and chive variation: Substitute one-third cup fresh lemon juice for the vinegar, and add a quarter cup of chopped fresh chives.
5. Fresh herb variation: Add three tablespoons chopped fresh herbs, such as parsley, tarragon, dill, or thyme.
6. Honey mustard variation: Substitute a tablespoon of honey mustard for the Dijon mustard. Add a tablespoon of chopped fresh herbs and three tablespoons of tomato ketchup.
7. Basil variation: Add a small minced clove of garlic and half a cup of packed fresh basil leaves. Put all the ingredients in a food processor or blender and blend until smooth.
