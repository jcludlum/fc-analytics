# Curry Spice Mix

Makes approximately 1/4 cup.

## Ingredients

- 1 tablespoon coriander seeds (2 teaspoons ground)
- 1 tablespoon fennel seeds (2 teaspoons ground)
- 1 teaspoon cumin seed (1 teaspoon ground)
- 4 cloves (1/4 teaspoon ground)
- 2 cardamom pods (1/4 teaspoon ground)
- 1/2-inch stick of cinnamon (1/2 teaspoon ground)
- 1/2 teaspoon peppercorns (1/2 teaspoon ground)
- 1/2 tablespoon ground turmeric
- 1 teaspoon ground cayenne pepper

*** = component recipe

## Directions

1. Put a frying pan over medium heat; when it's hot, add about a teaspoon of oil.
2. Once the oil is hot, add the whole spices (all but the turmeric and cayenne, which are usually sold already ground) and roast, stirring frequently, until they begin to brown and release their aroma. If using already-ground spices instead of whole, roast very briefly, about 15 seconds, in a dry frying pan over low heat, and be ready to remove them quickly since they burn fast.
3. Grind the roasted spices fine in a small coffee grinder (a blender or food processor won't work, since the blades sit too high to cut the small spices). Stir in the ground turmeric and cayenne pepper.
