# Coconut Milk

Makes 1 cup thick milk and 1 cup thin milk.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup shredded, unsweetened coconut
- 2 cups boiling water

*** = component recipe

## Directions

1. Soak 1 cup of the shredded coconut in 1 cup of the boiling water for 10 to 15 minutes.
2. Put the mixture in a blender or food processor and pulverize for 1 minute.
3. Line a bowl with cheesecloth and pour in the wet paste. Gather the edges of the cheesecloth and squeeze out all the liquid. This first batch is a rich, creamy, thick milk.
4. Put the squeezed coconut pulp through the same process again: soak it in the remaining cup of boiling water, pulverize, and strain through cheesecloth into another bowl. This second batch will be much thinner.
