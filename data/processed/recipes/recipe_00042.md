# Preserved Lemons

Preserved lemons were a revelation to me-they're versatile, delicious, and easy to prepare. Experiment with different combinations. For Mediterranean cooking, I prepare lemons with extra-virgin olive oil, oregano, bay leaves, and peppercorns. For Indian recipes, I blend limes and key limes with canola oil, paprika, cayenne, and black mustard seed. Makes about 3 quarts.

## Ingredients

- 12 medium to large lemons, unsprayed, if possible
- 2 Tbs. sea salt
- 2 Tbs. kosher salt
- Herbs and spices of your choice-dried chili pods, oregano, bay leaves, peppercorns
- 3 to 4 cups extra-virgin olive oil
- 3 to 4 cups canola oil

*** = component recipe

## Directions

1. Wash the lemons thoroughly. Slice each into eight wedges, or crosswise into 1/4-in. sections.
2. Arrange the lemons in a single layer on a tray or dish, sprinkle with salt, cover with plastic wrap, and repeat until all the lemons are laid out and salted. Let stand 24 hours (refrigerate in hot climates).
3. Layer the lemons into a glass clamp-lid jar with the herbs and spices of your choice, discarding the salty juice that has seeped from them.
4. Cover with the oil, submerging the fruit by at least 1/2 in. Give the jar a gentle rap to dislodge any air bubbles.
5. Keep the jar on the counter or in a cool pantry. After two weeks, the lemons should be soft-textured with a mellow-tart flavor.
6. Once you've started using them, refrigerate or store in a cool pantry, always maintaining a 1/2-in. layer of oil on top.
