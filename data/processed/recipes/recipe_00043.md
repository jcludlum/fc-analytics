# Asparagus with Orange Sauce

Freshly squeezed orange juice is an absolute must for this recipe; don't be tempted to substitute frozen concentrate. Use white pepper here if you can, to avoid black specks in the sauce. I also prefer the taste of white pepper. Serves six.

## Ingredients

- 3 lb. asparagus spears
- 1 Tbs. unsalted butter
- Salt
- freshly ground white pepper
- Zest of 1 orange
- Zest of 1 lemon
- 3 cups freshly squeezed orange juice (sweet blood orange if available)
- 2 tsp. French champagne vinegar
- 2 tsp. highest-quality, mild white-wine vinegar
- 1/4 cup (approximately) lemon juice
- Salt
- freshly ground white pepper

*** = component recipe

## Directions

1. Immerse the asparagus briefly in cold water, stirring and rolling the stalks to remove any sand lodged in the tips. Snap off the tough bottoms and peel the spears, then store them wrapped in a damp towel in the refrigerator. Set up the steamer.
2. With a vegetable peeler, remove the zests from the orange and lemon in strips. Slice the strips lengthwise into thin needles, blanch for 1 min. in boiling water, then drain, dry, and roll in plastic wrap.
3. Over low heat, reduce the orange juice by half in a heavy, large pan (watch closely, since it boils over easily).
4. Remove from the heat, add the vinegar, and taste-the juice will still be quite sweet. Add the lemon juice bit by bit until a pleasant balance is reached between sour and sweet. Season the sauce with salt and pepper to taste and reserve.
5. Just before serving, put the sauce over a low flame to reheat but not boil.
6. Steam the asparagus just until tender, 2 to 3 min. (less for very small spears, more for large ones). To test, cut a slice off the base of a stalk and taste; it's done when firm but not crunchy at the center.
7. Roll the asparagus quickly in the butter. Season lightly with salt and pepper.
8. Spoon the sauce onto hot individual dishes or a platter, arrange the asparagus on the sauce, and sprinkle with the slivers of zest.
