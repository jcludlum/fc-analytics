# Lamb Stock

This stock makes a flavorful base for a sauce to accompany many lamb dishes. You can prepare the stock the day before you plan to use it. Makes 2 cups.

## Ingredients

- 3 Tbs. olive oil
- Lamb scraps and bones from 3 loin racks
- 1 1/2 large onions, chopped coarse
- 3 large carrots, chopped coarse
- 6 cloves garlic, crushed but not peeled
- 72 sprigs fresh thyme or 7 tsp. dried thyme
- 6 sprigs Italian parsley
- 1 bay leaf
- Water

*** = component recipe

## Directions

1. Over medium to high heat in a large, heavy pot, heat the oil. Brown the bones and scraps, the onion, the carrot, and the garlic, stirring frequently to prevent scorching.
2. When the meat and bones are nicely browned, add the thyme, parsley, and bay leaf, and stir for a minute until you can smell the oils from the heating thyme.
3. Add cold water almost to cover the bones (5 to 6 cups should do it) and bring to a boil. Skim off any scum that rises to the surface, then lower the heat so the liquid just simmers.
4. Reduce slowly until only 2 cups of liquid remain. Discard the bones.
5. Strain the stock through a fine sieve, pressing with the back of a spoon to extract all the liquid.
6. After the stock cools, skim off and discard any grease that has risen to the top.
