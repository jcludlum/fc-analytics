# Almond-Pistachio Macaroons

The technique of combining almond paste and sugar is the same as for creaming butter and sugar, but almond paste is heavier than butter, so you must beat longer. Makes 32 macaroons.

## Ingredients

- 18 oz. (2 cups) almond paste
- 7 oz. (1 cup) granulated white sugar
- 7 oz. (2 cups) sifted confectioners' sugar
- 1/2 cup (3 to 4 large) egg whites
- 2 tsp. rosewater
- 2 tsp. vanilla extract
- 2 tsp. liqueur
- 1/8 tsp. salt
- 4 oz. (1 cup) unsalted, undyed chopped pistachios

*** = component recipe

## Directions

1. Heat the oven to 325°F. Line 2 or 3 good-quality cookie sheets with bakers' parchment.
2. Divide the almond paste into pieces (about 1/4 cup each) and put them in the bowl of a heavy-duty mixer fitted with a paddle (don't use a food processor; the batter is too heavy). Add the granulated sugar and quickly turn the mixer on and off a few times to draw the sugar into the mixture. Work on low speed until the mixture forms coarse, even crumbs-don't let it go beyond this point to a paste.
3. Turn off the mixer, add the confectioners' sugar, and mix on low speed for 1 min., then medium speed for 1 to 2 min., until very smooth and the mixture begins to compact around the sides of the bowl or when pinched. Scrape the sides and bottom of the bowl.
4. Add the egg whites, rosewater (or vanilla extract or liqueur), and salt. Mix on medium speed until combined but still moist and tacky, scraping down the bowl and paddle. Turn the mixer to medium-high speed for 2 min. or longer, until the mixture is light in texture and almost white in color.
5. Reduce to low speed, add half the pistachios, and mix until combined.
6. Use a #40 (1 1/2-in.) ice-cream scoop (or a heaping tablespoon) to place scoops of batter on the prepared cookie sheets, leaving room between each one.
7. Sprinkle each macaroon with the remaining pistachios and press down slightly so the nuts stick.
8. Bake for 25 to 28 min., until puffed and light and still a little soft but not wet, with bottoms very light brown, not dark.
9. Put the cookie sheets on a rack and let the macaroons cool completely before moving. Carefully peel the cookies from the parchment and store at room temperature in an airtight container.
