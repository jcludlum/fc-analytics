# Gulyás (Goulash) with Dumplings

This soup is similar to the original meal prepared by cattlemen on the Hungarian plains. If you can't find Hungarian bread, use the best Italian or French you can buy. When the soup is done, make tiny dumplings (galuska) to finish it off. We like to serve a red Hungarian wine, such as a Bikaver, with gulyas. Serves four.

## Ingredients

- 1 large red onion, diced or grated
- 2 Tbs. lard
- 2 Tbs. oil
- 2 lb. lean beef, cut into 3/4-in. cubes
- 2 tsp. paprika, more or less to taste
- 1 green pepper, sliced
- 2 tsp. red-pepper paste ***
- 1 tsp. salt
- 1 large potato, peeled and cut into chunks
- 1 large carrot, peeled and cut into chunks
- 1 large parsnip, peeled and cut into chunks
- 1 egg
- Salt
- About 1/2 cup flour

*** = component recipe

## Directions

1. In a deep pot, saute the onion in the lard or oil until it turns a light golden color.
2. Add the beef and brown it on all sides.
3. Add the paprika, a few slices of the green pepper, the red-pepper paste (or salt), and a little water to prevent burning. Cover and simmer for about 1 hour, checking and adding water periodically if needed.
4. Add the rest of the green pepper, the potato, carrot, and parsnip, along with as much water as you like depending on how much broth you want. Cover and cook until the meat is tender, about 30 min. Taste for salt.
5. Make the dumplings: in a small bowl or cup, beat the egg with a pinch of salt. Add enough flour to make a sticky paste and stir until perfectly smooth.
6. Take up a small amount of the paste-less than 1/4 tsp.-on the tip of a teaspoon and dip it into the boiling soup to release it. Continue until all the paste is used. Let boil another minute.
7. Serve in soup bowls with thick slices of Hungarian bread on the side.
