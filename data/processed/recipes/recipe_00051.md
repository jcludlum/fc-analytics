# Red-Pepper Paste

We like to use this paste in place of salt for the additional flavor and color it gives. Don't use iodized salt, which contributes a chemical taste.

## Ingredients

- 2 lb. red peppers, cleaned and cut into pieces
- 8 oz. noniodized salt

*** = component recipe

## Directions

1. Clean the peppers and cut them into pieces.
2. Puree the peppers to a paste in a food processor.
3. Mix in the salt, put into jars, and refrigerate. It will keep indefinitely. Use it with soups, gulyás, and paprikás dishes instead of salt.
