# Puff Pancake

Baking a puff pancake is a great way to season a skillet because nothing ever sticks to the pan, well lubricated as it is with butter. A puff pancake, traditional Saturday morning fare in our household, is simply a thin batter of eggs, flour, and milk that's baked in a sizzling hot skillet. In the oven, the edges of the pancake creep above the sides of the skillet and the center puffs dramatically. It comes out deliciously golden brown and crispy on the bottom. This is a perfect breakfast for two, and it's a cinch to whip up while the coffee's brewing. Serves two.

## Ingredients

- 2 eggs
- 1/2 cup flour
- 1/2 cup milk
- Dash of salt
- 4 tablespoons butter

*** = component recipe

## Directions

1. Set the oven to 450°F and put a large skillet or Dutch oven in to heat.
2. In a bowl, beat the eggs, flour, milk, and salt together all at once (mixing everything at once is important, or the pancake won't puff; the more air beaten into the batter, the higher it will rise).
3. When the oven is hot, toss the butter into the skillet and let it melt.
4. Pour the batter into the skillet and return it to the oven.
5. Bake for 12 to 15 min., or until the pancake is puffed and golden.
6. Cut in half and serve immediately with warm maple syrup.
