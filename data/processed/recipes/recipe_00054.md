# Fresh Summer Berry Granita

Makes about 5 cups.

## Ingredients

- 3 pints fresh ripe blackberries
- 3 pints fresh ripe raspberries
- 3 pints fresh ripe boysenberries
- Pinch of salt
- 1 Tbs. lemon juice
- 3/4 to 1 cup simple syrup ***

*** = component recipe

## Directions

1. Puree the berries with the salt and lemon juice.
2. Strain to remove the seeds (leave a quarter of the puree unstrained if you want some of the seeds for texture).
3. Combine with the simple syrup and freeze in a covered container for about 24 hours, until solid.
4. Let sit at room temperature about 20 minutes if needed to soften slightly, then scrape with a large spoon or ice-cream scoop into a chilled bowl to serve.
