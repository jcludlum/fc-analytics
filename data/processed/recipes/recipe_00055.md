# Peach-Champagne Granita

Makes about 6 cups.

## Ingredients

- 3 lb. ripe peaches (about 10) with skins
- Pinch of salt
- 1 Tbs. fresh lemon juice
- 3 Tbs. champagne
- 1 cup simple syrup ***

*** = component recipe

## Directions

1. Wash and pit the peaches. Put them in a nonreactive pot with the salt, lemon juice, and champagne.
2. Cook until soft, stirring often so the peaches don't scorch.
3. Cool, then puree in a food processor until smooth.
4. Combine with the simple syrup and freeze in a covered container for about 24 hours, until solid.
5. Let sit at room temperature about 20 minutes if needed to soften slightly, then scrape with a large spoon or ice-cream scoop into a chilled bowl to serve.
