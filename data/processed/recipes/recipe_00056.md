# Two Plum Granita

Follow the directions for the Peach-Champagne Granita, but use either of the following ingredient sets. Each makes about 5 cups.

## Ingredients

- 2 1/2 lb. green Kelsey plums
- Pinch of salt
- 1 Tbs. lemon juice
- 2 Tbs. Tuaca liqueur (or other orange liqueur)
- 3/4 cup simple syrup ***
- 2 1/2 lb. Italian prune plums
- Pinch of salt
- 1 Tbs. lemon juice
- 2 Tbs. sweet muscat wine
- 3/4 cup simple syrup ***

*** = component recipe

## Directions

1. Green Kelsey plum version: wash and pit the plums, then put them in a nonreactive pot with the salt, lemon juice, and Tuaca liqueur. Cook until soft, stirring often so the plums don't scorch. Cool, then puree in a food processor until smooth. Combine with the simple syrup.
2. Italian prune plum version: wash and pit the plums, then put them in a nonreactive pot with the salt, lemon juice, and sweet muscat wine. Cook until soft, stirring often so the plums don't scorch. Cool, then puree in a food processor until smooth. Combine with the simple syrup.
3. Freeze either mixture in a covered container for about 24 hours, until solid (stir occasionally if it shows signs of separating).
4. Let sit at room temperature about 20 minutes if needed to soften slightly, then scrape with a large spoon or ice-cream scoop into a chilled bowl to serve.
