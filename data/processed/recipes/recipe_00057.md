# Simple Syrup

Simple syrup keeps for up to three weeks. To make an herb-infused syrup, tie eight to ten large herb leaves or two or three sprigs of herbs in cheesecloth or a muslin infusion bag. Cook with the sugar and water, and then let steep no more than twenty minutes. Makes about 3 cups.

## Ingredients

- 2 1/2 cups water
- 1 3/4 cups sugar
- 8 to 10 large herb leaves or sprigs (optional, for an infused syrup)

*** = component recipe

## Directions

1. Put the water and sugar in a nonreactive pot and cook over medium-high heat, stirring frequently, until the mixture boils.
2. Cool, then store in a covered container in the refrigerator.
3. To make an infused syrup, tie the herb leaves or sprigs in cheesecloth or a muslin infusion bag and cook them with the sugar and water. Once the mixture boils, remove from the heat and let steep no more than twenty minutes, then remove the herbs.
