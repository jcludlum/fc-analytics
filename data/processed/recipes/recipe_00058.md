# Blueberry-Pomegranate Granita

Makes about 4 cups.

## Ingredients

- 4 to 5 cups fresh blueberries, washed and stemmed
- 2 Tbs. fresh pomegranate juice
- 1 Tbs. fresh lemon juice
- Pinch of salt
- 3/4 to 1 cup simple syrup ***

*** = component recipe

## Directions

1. In a nonreactive pot, cook the blueberries, pomegranate juice, lemon juice, and salt until the berries soften and begin to burst, releasing their color and flavor.
2. Puree when cool.
3. Combine with the simple syrup and freeze in a covered container for about 24 hours, until solid.
4. Let sit at room temperature about 20 minutes if needed to soften slightly, then scrape with a large spoon or ice-cream scoop into a chilled bowl to serve.
