# Mango Granita

Makes about 5 cups.

## Ingredients

- 6 ripe mangoes, peeled and seeded
- Pinch of salt
- 1 Tbs. fresh lemon juice
- 1 Tbs. fresh lime juice
- 1 cup simple syrup ***

*** = component recipe

## Directions

1. Puree the mangoes with the salt and lemon or lime juice until smooth.
2. Combine with the simple syrup and freeze in a covered container for about 24 hours, until solid (this one usually scrapes easily right out of the freezer).
3. Scrape with a large spoon or ice-cream scoop into a chilled bowl to serve.
