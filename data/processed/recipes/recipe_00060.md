# Roasted Rhubarb Granita

Makes about 5 cups.

## Ingredients

- 2 lb. rhubarb stalks, washed, trimmed, and cut into 1-in. pieces
- 1 cinnamon stick
- 1 3-in. branch rosemary
- 1 Tbs. fresh lemon juice
- Pinch of salt
- 1 cup rosemary-infused simple syrup ***

*** = component recipe

## Directions

1. Put the rhubarb in a nonreactive baking dish with the cinnamon stick and rosemary. Sprinkle with the lemon juice and a pinch of salt.
2. Cover with aluminum foil and roast in a 375°F oven for 40 min., or until the rhubarb is soft.
3. Remove the cinnamon stick and rosemary, then puree the rhubarb.
4. Combine with the rosemary-infused simple syrup and freeze in a covered container for about 24 hours, until solid.
5. Let sit at room temperature about 20 minutes if needed to soften slightly, then scrape with a large spoon or ice-cream scoop into a chilled bowl to serve.
