# Lamb, Lentil & Artichoke Salad

I like to use lamb loin in this salad: when roasted and sliced, it's tender, delicious, and visually appealing. Loin chops, served one per person, are a good substitute if you can't find a boneless loin. This salad also tastes great with leftover cooked lamb. Serves four.

## Ingredients

- 3/4 cup dried lentils
- 1 tsp. chopped fresh thyme
- 1/4 tsp. dried thyme leaves
- 1 tsp. chopped fresh parsley
- 1 tsp. chopped fresh sage
- 1/8 tsp. dried sage
- 1 tsp. chopped fresh rosemary
- 1/4 tsp. dried rosemary
- 1/2 tsp. minced fresh garlic
- 8 oz. boneless lamb loin
- 4 loin chops, 1-in. thick
- Salt
- pepper
- Oil
- 1/2 cup finely diced fennel
- 1/2 cup finely diced tomato
- 1/4 cup finely diced shallot
- Assorted salad greens-romaine, red and green oak leaf, mache, arugula, radicchio, endive, watercress
- 1 cup quartered marinated artichoke hearts
- Rosemary-Pepper Vinaigrette ***

*** = component recipe

## Directions

1. Put the lentils in a saucepan and cover with lightly salted cold water. Cover and simmer until tender, about 40 min. Rinse, drain, and reserve.
2. Mix the thyme, parsley, sage, rosemary, and garlic together and rub over the lamb. Season with salt and pepper, then let sit for 15 min. to absorb the flavor.
3. Grill or broil the lamb on a lightly oiled rack 3 to 4 min. per side until medium rare. Remove from the heat and keep warm.
4. Mix the lentils with the diced fennel, tomato, and shallot, and season to taste with salt and pepper. Heat the lentil mixture in a double boiler or microwave until warm.
5. Mound a quarter of the lentils in the center of each plate. Arrange the salad greens in a cluster behind the lentils (a radicchio leaf on top is a nice touch). Place the artichoke hearts in a semicircle around the front of the plate.
6. Cut the lamb into thin slices and fan atop the warm lentil mixture. Drizzle with the Rosemary-Pepper Vinaigrette.
