# Curried Lentil & Eggplant Cakes

You'll need about sixteen thin slices of eggplant, about three inches wide, so choose one large, evenly shaped eggplant, or two medium-sized ones. These cakes can be assembled in advance and baked right before serving. They make a nice first course accompanied by salad greens and chopped tomato. Serves four.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup dried lentils
- 1 large eggplant
- 2 medium eggplants
- Olive oil
- Lemon juice
- Salt
- 2 tsp. to 1 Tbs. curry powder
- 1 onion, diced
- 1 Tbs. red-wine vinegar
- 1 small garlic clove, minced
- 1 small tomato, peeled, seeded, and diced
- 1 Tbs. mixed chopped fresh herbs-thyme, rosemary, parsley, chives
- Fresh cilantro sprigs for decoration
- Cumin Vinaigrette ***

*** = component recipe

## Directions

1. Put the lentils in a saucepan and cover with lightly salted cold water. Cover and simmer gently until tender, about 40 min. Remove, rinse, and drain half the lentils and reserve. Continue cooking the rest until very soft, then drain well and puree.
2. Slice the eggplant lengthwise into 1/8-in. slices (an electric slicer or mandoline makes this easy).
3. Lightly coat the eggplant slices in a mixture of two parts olive oil to one part lemon juice, and sprinkle lightly with salt. Charbroil on a hot grill or broil in the oven until the eggplant has browned slightly, about 1 min. per side (it doesn't need to be thoroughly cooked at this point). Let cool.
4. Put the curry powder in a dry frying pan and toast lightly until it just begins to brown, about 30 seconds. Immediately add the diced onion and 1 Tbs. olive oil and cook over medium heat, stirring frequently, until the onion is soft and caramelized, 5 to 7 min.
5. Add the vinegar to the pan and deglaze, scraping to dissolve what remains of the onion and curry powder. Add the whole lentils, lentil puree, garlic, tomato, and herbs. Salt to taste and cook until heated through.
6. Coat four 8-oz. molds with butter or oil. Line each mold with about four slices of eggplant so the inside is completely covered and the edges hang over the mold.
7. Fill each mold with a quarter of the lentil mixture and fold over the eggplant ends to cover.
8. Bake the molds in a 350°F oven for 20 min., or until thoroughly heated.
9. Turn out each cake onto a warmed plate, drizzle with the Cumin Vinaigrette, and decorate with a few sprigs of fresh cilantro.
