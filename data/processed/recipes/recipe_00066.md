# Pepper-Coated Salmon with Lentils

In my restaurant, I finish this dish with a pinot noir sauce that I make with rich veal stock. For a quicker home version, you can round out the flavors of the salmon and lentils with a squirt of lemon and a few drops of extra-virgin olive oil, and accompany the dish with a glass of pinot noir. Serves four.

## Ingredients

- 3/4 cup dried lentils
- 1/2 cup black peppercorns
- 4 6-oz. salmon fillets, approximately 1 1/2-in. thick
- Salt
- 2 Tbs. olive oil
- 1/2 cup finely diced fennel
- 1/2 cup finely diced carrot
- 1/2 cup finely diced celery
- 1/2 cup finely diced leek
- 1/2 cup finely diced onion
- 1 tomato, peeled, seeded, and finely diced
- 1 Tbs. chopped mixed fresh herbs-thyme, rosemary, parsley, and chives
- Extra-virgin olive oil
- Lemon juice

*** = component recipe

## Directions

1. Put the lentils in a saucepan and cover with lightly salted cold water. Cover and simmer until tender, about 40 min. Rinse, drain, and reserve.
2. Coarsely crack the black peppercorns on a wooden cutting board with a mallet or the back of a heavy frying pan. Lightly salt the salmon fillets, then generously coat them with the pepper, pressing lightly to stick.
3. Heat 1 Tbs. of the olive oil in a frying pan until very hot. Add the fennel, carrot, celery, leek, and onion, and sauté until translucent and starting to brown. Add the tomato, herbs, and lentils, and season with salt. Remove from the heat and keep warm.
4. Heat the remaining 1 Tbs. olive oil in a frying pan and sauté the salmon over high heat for about 3 min. per side, until only slightly translucent inside.
5. Divide the warm lentil mixture among four warmed plates. Top each with a salmon fillet and drizzle with the extra-virgin olive oil and lemon juice.
