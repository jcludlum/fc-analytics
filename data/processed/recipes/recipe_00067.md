# Cumin Vinaigrette

Makes 1 cup.

## Ingredients

- 1 Tbs. chopped fresh cilantro
- 2 cloves roasted garlic
- 1 clove raw garlic
- 2 Tbs. fresh lemon juice
- 1/4 cup red-wine vinegar
- 1 Tbs. ground cumin
- 1/2 cup extra-virgin olive oil
- 1/4 cup canola oil
- 1/4 cup vegetable oil
- 1 Tbs. brown sugar
- Salt

*** = component recipe

## Directions

1. Combine the cilantro, garlic, lemon juice, and vinegar and puree well in a blender or food processor. Strain through a fine mesh sieve.
2. Put the cumin in a dry frying pan and toast lightly, stirring frequently, until it just begins to brown. Add to the vinegar mixture.
3. Whisk in the oils and brown sugar, and salt to taste. Mix the vinaigrette immediately before using, since it won't remain emulsified for long.
