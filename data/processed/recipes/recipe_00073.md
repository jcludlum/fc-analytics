# Roasted Eggplant with Shrimp & Basil

The smoky, roasted flavor of the eggplant is a nice foil to the basil in this dish. Long, slender Thai or Japanese eggplant work best because they cook quickly, have fewer seeds, and taste less bitter than the more common plump eggplant. Serves six.

## Ingredients

- 6 Thai or Japanese eggplant, 6 to 8 in. long
- 1/3 cup vegetable oil
- 1 Tbs. minced garlic
- 4 shallots, sliced thin
- 1/2 cup chopped cilantro
- 9 small raw shrimp, peeled, deveined, and halved
- 1/2 cup water
- 1 tsp. brown fermented bean sauce
- miso
- 2 Tbs. soy sauce
- fish sauce
- 3 to 4 chile peppers (Thai, serrano, or jalapeno), seeded and cut in fine julienne
- 1 small red bell pepper, julienned
- 1 Tbs. sugar
- 2 cups loosely packed basil leaves (Thai or sweet)

*** = component recipe

## Directions

1. Char the skins of each whole eggplant under a broiler, on a grill, or in a frying pan, turning as each side blackens. When cool enough to handle, pull the skin off.
2. Slice each eggplant lengthwise halfway through. Spread open the sides of each eggplant to form a container and arrange them on a serving plate.
3. Heat the oil in a wok or large, heavy frying pan over medium heat. Add the garlic and stir until it just begins to color. Stir in the shallots and 1 Tbs. of the cilantro.
4. Add the shrimp and water and stir well. Stir in the fermented bean sauce (or miso), soy sauce (or fish sauce), chile peppers, red pepper, sugar, and basil.
5. Lower the heat and simmer briefly, until the shrimp is cooked, about 2 min.
6. Pour the shrimp and sauce over the eggplant. Garnish with the remaining cilantro.
