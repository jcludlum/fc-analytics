# Cucumber Salad

## Ingredients

- 1/4 cup sugar
- 1 tsp. salt
- 1/2 cup rice vinegar
- 4 pickling cucumbers, quartered lengthwise, seeded, and sliced thin
- 2 regular cucumbers, quartered lengthwise, seeded, and sliced thin
- 1 shallot, sliced thin
- A few whole cilantro leaves
- A few strips of finely julienned red bell pepper (1/2 in. long)

*** = component recipe

## Directions

1. In a small saucepan, simmer the sugar and salt in the rice vinegar until dissolved, about 5 min.
2. Let the mixture cool to room temperature and stir in the cucumbers and shallot.
3. Sprinkle cilantro leaves and red pepper on top to decorate.
