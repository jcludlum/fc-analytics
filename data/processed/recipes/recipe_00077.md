# Basic Pizza Dough

Yields about 3 pounds of dough.

## Ingredients

- 1 tablespoon dry yeast
- 1/2 teaspoon sugar
- 2 2/3 cups warm water
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 7 1/2 cups unbleached all-purpose flour
- 1/4 cup whole-wheat flour
- 1/4 cup rye flour
- 1 tablespoon coarse salt
- 2 teaspoons fine salt

*** = component recipe

## Directions

1. Proof the yeast for 5 to 10 minutes in 1 cup of the warm water with the sugar, until the yeast dissolves and the liquid begins to appear creamy.
2. Add the remaining water and 1 1/2 to 2 cups of the flour (including the whole-wheat or rye flour). Beat well (about a hundred strokes) until smooth and soupy, then let stand for 10 to 15 minutes, until bubbly and swollen.
3. Add the salt and olive oil, then stir in the rest of the flour by the cupful until you get a stiff but still slightly sticky dough, always stirring in the same direction.
4. When the dough begins to form a cohesive mass thick enough to hold its shape, turn it out onto a lightly floured surface and let it rest while you clean and oil the bowl.
5. Knead the dough, turning it clockwise by quarter turns and sprinkling a little flour on top and underneath before folding it over, adding just enough flour so the dough doesn't stick and tear. Kneading takes about 5 to 8 minutes.
6. When the dough is smooth, springy, and pliant (earlobe-soft), return it to the oiled bowl, cover with a damp cloth, and let it rise until doubled, about 35 to 45 minutes at 70° to 75°F.
7. After the first rise, either form and bake the crust immediately; punch the dough down and let it rise again before baking; or refrigerate it for several hours or up to two days, giving it a final punch down after about 40 minutes of chilling and storing it in a plastic bag. A third of the dough makes one 12- to 16-inch round crust.
