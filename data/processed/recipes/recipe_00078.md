# Asparagus, Prosciutto & Fontina Pizza

This is great on a thin, crisp crust.

## Ingredients

- 1/3 recipe Basic Pizza Dough, shaped into a thin crust ***
- Olive oil, for brushing
- 4 scallions, sliced
- 1/2 tsp. dill
- Salt
- pepper
- 8 to 10 spears asparagus, blanched, cooled, and drained
- about 2 oz. prosciutto, cut or torn into pieces
- about 5 oz. fontina cheese, sliced
- Parmesan, for sprinkling

*** = component recipe

## Directions

1. Brush the prepared dough lightly with olive oil. Sprinkle with the scallions, dill, salt, and pepper.
2. Bake at 475°F for 10 min., until the crust is firm and just beginning to color.
3. Remove from the oven and arrange the asparagus, prosciutto, and fontina cheese on the crust. Sprinkle with Parmesan.
4. Bake 8 to 10 min. more, or until the cheeses are melted and bubbly and the crust is golden.
