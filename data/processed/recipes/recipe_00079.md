# Shrimp, Mussels & Hot Pepper Pizza

This combination is especially good on a risen crust.

## Ingredients

- 1/3 recipe Basic Pizza Dough, shaped into a risen crust ***
- 6 shrimp, peeled and deveined, cut in half lengthwise
- Lemon juice
- Salt
- pepper
- 8 mussels, cleaned
- 1/2 cup seasoned tomato sauce
- 2 to 3 cloves garlic, slivered
- 1/2 tsp. oregano
- 1/2 chopped jalapeno pepper
- 1/2 cup shredded Monterey Jack
- 1/2 cup shredded mozzarella
- 2 Tbs. grated Parmesan
- Cilantro, for garnish

*** = component recipe

## Directions

1. Peel and devein the shrimp, and cut them in half lengthwise. Season with lemon juice, salt, and pepper.
2. Clean the mussels and steam them briefly, just long enough for them to open, about 1 min. Shell them.
3. Spread the tomato sauce thin over the prepared dough and sprinkle with the garlic and oregano.
4. Bake at 475°F for 12 to 15 min., until the crust begins to color.
5. Remove the pizza from the oven. Arrange the shrimp and mussels evenly over the crust and sprinkle with the jalapeno, Monterey Jack or mozzarella, and Parmesan.
6. Bake for 8 to 10 min. more, until the cheeses are melted and bubbly. Garnish with cilantro.
