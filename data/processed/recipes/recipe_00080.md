# Spinach, Mushroom & Blue Cheese Pizza

Try this on a thick and chewy crust.

## Ingredients

- 1/3 recipe Basic Pizza Dough, shaped into a thick, chewy crust ***
- Olive oil, for brushing
- 6 cloves garlic, slivered
- Salt
- pepper
- 10 oz. spinach, washed and stemmed
- red onion, thinly sliced
- mushrooms, thinly sliced
- 2 Tbs. pine nuts
- 2 Tbs. walnut pieces
- 1 cup grated mozzarella
- 1/2 cup crumbled blue cheese

*** = component recipe

## Directions

1. Brush the prepared dough lightly with olive oil and sprinkle with the garlic, salt, and pepper.
2. Bake at 475°F for 10 min.
3. Meanwhile, put the spinach in a colander in the sink and pour about a quart of boiling water over it. Let drain, then press gently to remove excess moisture without squeezing the spinach.
4. Remove the crust from the oven and arrange the spinach, red onion, mushrooms, and pine nuts or walnuts on it.
5. Bake 8 min.
6. Sprinkle on the mozzarella and blue cheese and bake another 5 min., until the cheeses are melted.
