# Sauté of Cod with Capers & Onions

All the ingredients in this dish are easy to come by, so you don't have to wait for a special occasion to make it. Cod is one of my favorite types of fish, and it doesn't get the credit it deserves. Fresh, good-quality cod is sweet and mild, with firm white flakes. Use fillets or steaks for this recipe. I would serve this dish with a fruity sauvignon blanc. Serves four.

## Ingredients

- 4 cod fillets or steaks, about 6 oz. each
- Salt
- pepper
- 1 Tbs. olive oil
- 6 Tbs. butter
- 2 large onions, sliced thin
- 1 cup dry white wine
- 3/4 cup reduced-salt chicken stock
- 2 small sour pickles (cornichons), cut in julienne
- 1 Tbs. drained capers
- 1 Tbs. chopped fresh parsley

*** = component recipe

## Directions

1. Rinse the cod and pat dry. Season with salt and pepper. (If using cod steaks, leave the skin and bone intact during cooking to help the fish hold together; after cooking, peel off the skin and pull out the bone.)
2. Heat the olive oil and 2 Tbs. of the butter in a large sauté pan. When the fat is hot, add the fish and sauté over medium-high heat until light brown, about 3 min. on each side. Remove the fish with a spatula and keep it warm.
3. Pour off all but 1 Tbs. of the fat and add the sliced onions. Cook over medium heat, stirring frequently, until soft and caramelized, 8 to 10 min.
4. Add the wine and stock and a little salt and pepper. Boil, scraping the bottom of the pan, until the liquid is reduced by about half and has a slightly syrupy consistency.
5. Put the fish and any accumulated juices back in the pan and cook for another minute or so, basting the fish with the sauce.
6. Add the remaining 4 Tbs. butter and shake the pan until it's blended into the sauce (do not boil, or the butter will separate and the sauce will be oily). Add the julienned pickle, capers, and parsley.
7. Arrange the fish on warmed individual plates or a platter and spoon the sauce over the fish.
