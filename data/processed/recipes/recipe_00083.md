# Crookneck Squash with Creole Vegetable Stuffing

Serves four.

## Ingredients

- 8 small (3-ounce) crookneck squash
- 4 large (6-ounce) crookneck squash
- 2 cups all-purpose vegetable stock, or use a stock cube ***
- 1/8 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 cup pearl barley
- 2 tablespoons unsalted butter
- 1 1/2 teaspoon finely chopped garlic
- 3/4 cup chopped onion
- 1/2 cup finely diced carrot
- 1/8 teaspoon ground nutmeg
- 1/8 teaspoon ground cayenne
- 1/2 cup finely diced celery
- 1/2 cup finely diced green pepper
- 3/4 cup fresh corn kernels
- 1 teaspoon finely chopped fresh rosemary
- 1 teaspoon chopped fresh thyme
- 1/4 teaspoon Tabasco, more to taste
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- Tomato and Pink-Grapefruit Sauce, for serving ***

*** = component recipe

## Directions

1. Wash the squash, blot dry, and trim the dry stem ends. Place each squash on the counter and let it roll into its natural resting position; cut a thick slice from the long side that's now parallel to the counter to form a lid.
2. With the tip of a paring knife, carefully score a line 1/4 inch in from the edge of the cut face to make a border. Cut several slashes across the surface, then scoop out the pulp with a melon baller or small spoon to form a hollow container. Repeat with the other squash and lids. Chop the pulp fine and set aside.
3. Steam the squash in a covered steamer, 6 to 7 minutes for the containers and 2 to 3 minutes for the lids. Immediately immerse them in a large bowl of ice water to stop the cooking, then remove, drain, and pat dry.
4. Bring 1 1/4 cups of the stock and the 1/8 tsp. salt to a boil. Add the barley, cover, and simmer until the stock is absorbed and the barley is plump and tender, 30 to 40 minutes. Set aside.
5. Heat the butter in a large frying pan until just foaming, add the garlic and onion, and sauté over medium-high heat until the onion turns clear. Add the carrot, nutmeg, and cayenne and continue cooking until the carrot is slightly soft and the onion begins to caramelize.
6. Stir in the reserved squash pulp, celery, green pepper, corn, and rosemary, and cook until the vegetables soften, another 3 to 5 minutes.
7. Add the remaining stock, the barley, thyme, Tabasco, and the 1/4 tsp. salt. Cook, stirring frequently, until all the ingredients are tender and the stock has reduced and been absorbed, 10 to 12 minutes. Season with salt to taste and remove from the heat.
8. Fill each squash container with the stuffing, packing firmly with a spoon but taking care not to tear the sides. If you like, set the lids back on top, or serve them alongside topped with a spoonful of stuffing.
9. Place all the filled squash and lids on a baking tray, cover with foil, and bake at 400°F until heated through, about 15 minutes. (If stuffed ahead and refrigerated, reheat at 375°F for 35 to 45 minutes.)
10. Serve with the Tomato and Pink-Grapefruit Sauce.
