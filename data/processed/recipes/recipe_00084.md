# Tomato & Pink-Grapefruit Sauce

In this easy recipe, pink grapefruit is an unexpected ingredient that adds a lot of fruitiness and a nice bitter edge. Rice-wine vinegar adds another sweet-sharp note. (If you don't have rice-wine vinegar, use a drop of honey and white-wine vinegar instead.) Makes about 3 cups.

## Ingredients

- 5 tablespoons unsalted butter
- 1/2 teaspoon finely chopped garlic
- 1 tablespoon rice-wine vinegar
- Juice from 1 pink grapefruit
- 2 pounds tomatoes, peeled, seeded, and chopped, about 2 1/2 cups
- Peeled segments from 1 pink grapefruit, cut in 1/2-inch chunks
- Pinch ground cayenne
- 1/2 teaspoon fresh thyme
- Salt

*** = component recipe

## Directions

1. In a large frying pan (preferably not cast iron), heat 2 tablespoons of the butter and sauté the garlic until golden.
2. Add the vinegar and grapefruit juice, and simmer over high heat until reduced by half, 2 to 3 minutes.
3. Add the chopped tomato and the grapefruit pieces and cook over medium heat for about 5 minutes.
4. Turn off the heat, add the cayenne and thyme, then whisk in the remaining butter. Add salt to taste.
