# All-Purpose Vegetable Stock

Makes 1 quart. All your accumulated scraps, including, or plus, the following ingredients. Good items to collect for all-purpose stock include corn cobs and husks, snow-pea strings, tiny garlic cloves too small for peeling and chopping, tomato skins and seeds, eggplant skins, clean potato parings, lettuce, and bits of apple or pear. Throw away woody stems, rough peelings, hard seeds, and blemished, gritty, or too-old pieces.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups coarsely chopped onion
- 1 cup coarsely chopped celery
- 2 cups coarsely chopped crookneck squash
- 2 corn cobs, cut in pieces
- 1 3/4 cups coarsely chopped carrot
- 1 cup seeded and coarsely chopped green pepper
- 1 cup coarsely chopped tomato
- 1/8 tsp. black peppercorns
- 1/8 tsp. coriander seed
- 1/8 tsp. fennel seed
- 10 stems parsley
- 10 stems cilantro
- 10 stems thyme
- 1 bay leaf
- 3 cloves garlic
- 3 quarts water

*** = component recipe

## Directions

1. Combine all the ingredients in a large stockpot.
