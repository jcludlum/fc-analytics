# Tokay Grape Butter

Look for Tokay grapes, which are mottled red over green, during the grape-harvest season. Here in California that's early September and October. Or use any grape with an interesting, distinctive flavor. Yields three 8-ounce jars.

## Ingredients

- 4 lb. grapes
- Water
- Sugar

*** = component recipe

## Directions

1. Wash and stem the grapes.
2. Add no more than 1 cup of water and cook until tender and collapsed.
3. Process through a food mill or sieve.
4. Weigh the pulp and add 8 oz. of sugar for each pound of pulp.
5. Cook until thick and creamy, stirring often and skimming the scum that rises while boiling.
6. Hot-pack in canning jars.
