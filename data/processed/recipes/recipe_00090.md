# Damson Cheese

Yields three to four 8-ounce jars.

## Ingredients

- 4 lb. damsons
- Water
- Sugar
- 1/2 tsp. almond extract

*** = component recipe

## Directions

1. Remove stems and leaves and wash the damsons. Squeeze to express juice, adding less than a cup of water if needed to prevent sticking.
2. Cook until tender.
3. Process through a food mill or sieve.
4. Weigh the pulp and add 14 oz. of sugar to each pound of pulp, along with the almond extract.
5. Cook, stirring frequently and skimming the scum that rises while boiling, until a spoon drawn through the mixture leaves a clean line behind it on the bottom of the pan.
6. Hot-pack into canning jars, or pour into molds or small terrines painted with flavorless oil or food-grade glycerin.
