# Peach Butter

Yields three to four 8-ounce jars.

## Ingredients

- 4 lb. peaches
- 1 to 2 cups water
- Sugar

*** = component recipe

## Directions

1. Slice the peaches, discarding the stones. (If the skins are thick and fuzzy, blanch and peel them before slicing.)
2. Cook with 1 to 2 cups of water, depending on the juiciness of the fruit, until very soft.
3. Process through a food mill or sieve.
4. Weigh the pulp and add 8 oz. of sugar for each pound of pulp.
5. Cook until thick and creamy, stirring often and skimming the scum that rises while boiling.
6. Hot-pack in canning jars.
