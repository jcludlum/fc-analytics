# Plum Butter

Yields three to four 8-ounce jars.

## Ingredients

- 4 lb. plums
- Water
- Sugar

*** = component recipe

## Directions

1. Wash and stem the plums. Crush a few to produce enough juice to prevent sticking, adding a little water if needed.
2. Cover and cook until tender and collapsed.
3. Process through a food mill or sieve.
4. Weigh the pulp and add 8 oz. of sugar for each pound of pulp.
5. Cook until thick and creamy, stirring often and skimming the scum that rises while boiling.
6. Hot-pack in canning jars.
