# Prune Butter

Yields six 8-ounce jars.

## Ingredients

- 4 lb. pitted prunes
- Water
- Sugar
- Juice and grated rind of 1 lemon

*** = component recipe

## Directions

1. Soak the pitted prunes overnight in water to cover.
2. Simmer in the same water until tender.
3. Process through a food mill or sieve.
4. Weigh the pulp and add 8 oz. of sugar for each pound of pulp, along with the lemon juice and grated rind.
5. Cook until thick and creamy, stirring often and skimming the scum that rises while boiling.
6. Hot-pack in canning jars.
