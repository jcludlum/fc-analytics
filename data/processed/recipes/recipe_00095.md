# Jerky Turkey

I usually jerk turkey over peachwood or applewood coals, but here's a method for the oven that's easy and tastes great. Brining the turkey for several hours flavors the meat a little, and helps the smoke and marinade flavors penetrate the meat during cooking. The roasting process mellows the intense heat of the peppers, and renders the jerk pleasantly spicy and aromatic. The leftovers make delicious sandwiches or fantastic tacos. If there's any fat in the turkey breast cavity, you can chop it up and mix it with the jerk rub; alternatively, use a few tablespoons of softened butter. Serves twelve.

## Ingredients

- 1 11-lb. bone-in turkey breast
- 1 1/2 to 2 cups jerk rub ***
- Salt
- pepper
- 1 cup dried hot chiles
- 2 cups brown sugar
- 1 cup sea salt
- 1 cup kosher salt
- 4-in. piece fresh ginger, peeled and chopped
- 2 Tbs. whole allspice
- 1/2 cup dried thyme
- 3 gal. cold water

*** = component recipe

## Directions

1. Combine all the brine ingredients in a large, nonreactive container and stir until the sugar and salt have dissolved.
2. Submerge the turkey breast in the brine, refrigerate, and let it soak at least two but not much more than eight hours.
3. Remove the turkey from the brine and carefully work your hand under the breast skin to separate it from the meat.
4. Apply the jerk rub under the skin, massaging it evenly over the breast, all the way to the other end.
5. Set the turkey in a roasting pan and dust lightly with salt and pepper.
6. Roast in a 350°F oven for about 3 hours, basting every 30 min. or so with the pan drippings, until the turkey is firm to the touch (or registers 165°F on a meat thermometer).
7. Let the turkey rest for about 10 min. before cutting the breast from the bone. Cut one breast side completely away from the bone, then carve it crosswise so every slice has a strip of crisp skin with a layer of jerk beneath.
