# Yogurt Cheese

Yogurt cheese is easy to make and versatile, delicious alone or mixed into other dishes. It has a creamy texture, even when made with nonfat yogurt, and because of that, it's been hailed as a "miracle" substitute for high-fat cream cheese, mayonnaise, and sour cream.

## Ingredients

- Plain yogurt, without gelatin
- Salt (about 1 tsp. per quart of yogurt)
- Fresh herbs, curry powder, minced garlic, or chopped black olives (optional flavorings)

*** = component recipe

## Directions

1. Choose a yogurt that tastes good to you plain, avoiding any that contains gelatin (gelatin inhibits the whey from draining); pectin is fine.
2. If using flavorings, stir them into the yogurt first, along with the salt, before draining-this lets the flavors ripen and makes a firm cheese easier to shape.
3. Line a funnel or colander with several thicknesses of cheesecloth or an unbleached coffee filter, and set it over a large measuring cup or jar. Spoon in the yogurt.
4. Cover and drain in the refrigerator. After an hour or two, the cheese will have a creamy consistency like sour cream; for a thick cheese that can be shaped and sliced, drain at least 8 and up to 24 hours.
5. Remove the cheese from the filter. Mold it into a log by rolling it up in plastic wrap or wax paper, or roll it between your palms into canapé-size balls. Keep refrigerated, and eat within a week.
