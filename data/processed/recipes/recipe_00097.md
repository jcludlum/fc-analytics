# Lobster Cakes with Basil & Lobster Coulis

These delicate lobster cakes look and taste wonderful served with fresh basil on a pool of lobster coulis. If you don't have time to make the coulis, serve the lobster cakes with mustard and fresh tarragon, or with a soft, homemade mayonnaise mixed with capers and parsley or chives. Serves four as a main course, up to twelve as an appetizer.

## Ingredients

- 4 live lobsters, 1 to 1 1/2 lb. each
- 1 medium shallot, minced fine
- 1 egg white
- 1/2 tsp. salt (preferably sea salt)
- Cayenne pepper
- 2 Tbs. Armagnac
- 2 Tbs. other brandy
- 1/4 cup heavy cream
- 1/2 cup basil leaves sliced in thin chiffonade, plus whole perfect leaves for decoration
- 2 Tbs. clarified unsalted butter
- Lobster Coulis (made from the reserved shells), for serving ***

*** = component recipe

## Directions

1. Have ready a bath of ice and water in a sink or large container. Kill the lobsters by immersing them in a pot of rapidly boiling water for 2 min. Submerge them in the ice water and chill completely.
2. Shell the lobster tails, claws, and knuckles. Force the meat from the lobster legs by rolling a wine bottle or rolling pin over them. Reserve the bodies and shells for the Lobster Coulis.
3. Put the tail meat of two of the lobsters into the work bowl of a food processor with the shallot, egg white, salt, and a pinch of cayenne. Process until very smooth. Add the Armagnac. While processing, add the cream in a steady stream, pausing occasionally to scrape down the work bowl.
4. Dice the remaining tail, claw, and knuckle meat into 1/4-in. pieces. In a large bowl, gently combine the diced lobster and the lobster mousse. Chill at least an hour while you prepare the Lobster Coulis with the reserved shells and bodies.
5. Reheat the finished Lobster Coulis and add the sliced basil to it.
6. Brush a nonstick skillet with a thin film of the clarified butter and spoon the lobster mixture into the pan to make twelve cakes (in batches if necessary). Brown on both sides over high heat, then reduce the heat and continue cooking 4 min., or until the cakes resist a gentle press.
7. At the last minute, add the lobster leg meat to the pan to warm.
8. Divide the cakes among serving plates lined with the basil lobster coulis. Arrange the leg meat over the cakes. Decorate with whole basil leaves.
