# Lobster Coulis

## Ingredients

- The body shells and white body frames of two lobsters
- 1/4 cup olive oil
- 4 cloves garlic, chopped coarse
- 1 medium leek (white part only), split, washed, and chopped
- 1/2 cup sliced carrot
- 1/4 cup chopped celery ribs
- 1 Tbs. fresh tarragon leaves
- 2 Tbs. chopped flat-leaf parsley leaves
- 1 tsp. chopped fresh thyme leaves
- 2 bay leaves
- 1/2 cup Armagnac
- 1/2 cup other brandy
- 1 cup dry white wine
- 2 lb. fresh plum tomatoes, peeled, seeded, and diced
- Salt (preferably sea salt)
- cayenne pepper

*** = component recipe

## Directions

1. Keep the lobster shells in large pieces for easy removal later, but separate the white body frames from the outer shells.
2. Put the shells, the body frames, and the oil in a large, nonreactive pot or Dutch oven. Sauté 5 min. over medium heat, stirring frequently to avoid scorching.
3. Add the garlic, vegetables (except the tomatoes), and herbs. Continue cooking until the carrots are tender, about 10 min.
4. Add the Armagnac and wine. Simmer to reduce by half.
5. Add the diced tomatoes and simmer 10 min.
6. Remove the lobster shells and bodies with tongs or a slotted spoon, and continue to reduce the sauce until slightly thickened over medium heat, about 10 min. (tomatoes with high water content will require a longer reduction time).
7. Cool slightly, then puree in a blender or food processor. Season with salt and a pinch of cayenne.
