# Stuffed Boneless Pork Loin

Serves six to eight.

## Ingredients

- 2 to 2 1/2-pound boneless pork loin
- Salt
- pepper
- Fresh rosemary and thyme, chopped
- Oil
- 1 pound mixed trimmed and cut vegetables (about 5 cups), choose from: small mushrooms in quarters, red/yellow/green peppers in medium julienne, yellow squash in half-rounds, zucchini in half-rounds, carrots in thin slices, eggplant in peeled half-rounds, scallions in 2-inch lengths
- 1 tablespoon chopped shallot
- 1 teaspoon chopped garlic
- 2 tablespoons chopped mixed fresh herbs-parsley, thyme, rosemary, and oregano
- Salt
- pepper
- 1/4 cup olive oil

*** = component recipe

## Directions

1. Mix the vegetables, shallot, garlic, herbs, and salt and pepper together with about 3 tablespoons of the olive oil and leave to marinate about 30 minutes at room temperature.
2. Heat another tablespoon of the olive oil in a very hot skillet, add the vegetables, and cook over high heat for 2 to 3 minutes, stirring or shaking the pan constantly, until nicely caramelized.
3. Spread the seared vegetables on a sheet pan and finish cooking in a 400°F oven for another 5 to 10 minutes, just until tender.
4. Let the cooked stuffing cool, then spoon it down the center of a double thickness of plastic wrap in a line about as long as the pork loin. Fold over one side of the plastic and shape the stuffing into a loose log about 1 1/2 inches in diameter, then roll it completely in the plastic. Twist one end closed and tie it with string, then twist the other end until the log is tight and tie that end too. Freeze the stuffing log until solid.
5. Trim the fat from the pork loin as desired. To butterfly it, make one straight cut along one long edge, cutting to within about half the meat's thickness of the other long side, so that when opened out, the whole piece is an even thickness. Keep the whole edge of the knife blade in contact with the meat and make one continuous pulling stroke rather than sawing.
6. Open the meat like a book and lay it flat. If there are uneven spots, put the meat between two pieces of plastic wrap and gently pound them out.
7. Season the meat with salt and pepper, unwrap the frozen stuffing log, and place it in the center of the meat. Wrap the meat around the stuffing, butting the two long edges together in a neat seam with a little extra meat for slack, since the meat will shrink slightly as it cooks.
8. Truss the meat to keep the stuffing secure and help the roast hold a straight shape during cooking. Roll it in the chopped rosemary and thyme and season with salt and pepper.
9. Heat a little more oil in a skillet and sear the roast over medium-high heat until browned on all sides, turning as needed. Transfer the meat to a rack in a roasting pan.
10. Roast in a 375°F oven for 15 to 20 minutes per pound, until a meat thermometer inserted in the meat (not the stuffing) reads about 145°F (it will rise to about 150°F as it rests).
11. Let the roast rest in a warm place for about 15 minutes before carving into slices.
