# Basic Shortcake Biscuits

Makes 8 biscuits cut with a 3-inch round cutter.

## Ingredients

- 12 ounces (2 2/3 cups) all-purpose flour
- 2 tablespoons sugar
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 tablespoon plus 2 teaspoons baking powder
- 4 ounces (2/3 cup) vegetable shortening, chilled
- 1 cup buttermilk, chilled
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract

*** = component recipe

## Directions

1. Combine the flour, sugar, salt, and baking powder in a bowl and stir to mix.
2. Add the shortening and incorporate it into the flour mixture by taking a big pinch of the ingredients and rubbing your fingertips together lightly, working until only very small lumps of fat remain, nothing larger than a small pea.
3. Combine the chilled buttermilk and vanilla extract, add to the flour mixture, and mix with your hand just until the flour is completely moistened and the ingredients form a loose dough. Don't overmix, or the shortcakes will be tough.
4. Pat out the dough onto a floured surface and roll it out until about 1/2 inch thick. Cut into rounds with a 3-inch biscuit cutter, dipping the cutter in flour between cuts to prevent sticking. Reroll the scraps once and cut again (rerolling more than once toughens the dough).
5. Arrange the biscuits about an inch apart on a parchment-lined sheet pan and bake in a preheated 400°F oven for 15 to 20 minutes, until very lightly colored and about doubled in height.
6. Remove from the oven, peel off the parchment, and let cool on a rack.
