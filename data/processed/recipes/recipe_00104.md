# Basic Berry Filling (for shortcake)

Enough for 8 biscuits.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup strawberries
- 1 cup blackberries
- 1 cup raspberries
- 1/4 cup sugar, or to taste

*** = component recipe

## Directions

1. Rinse the berries and slice any larger ones into small pieces, picking out any seeds if needed.
2. Put the fruit in a non-aluminum bowl and sprinkle with the sugar. Toss lightly to mix.
3. Cover and let sit for about an hour at room temperature, so the sugar draws out the natural juices. Taste, and add a tablespoon or two of fresh lemon juice if the mixture tastes flat.
