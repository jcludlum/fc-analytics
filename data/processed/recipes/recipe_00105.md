# Basic Whipped Cream

Enough for 8 biscuits.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups heavy cream
- 1/4 cup sugar, or to taste
- 1 tablespoon vanilla extract

*** = component recipe

## Directions

1. Start with a clean, cold, stainless-steel, glass, or ceramic bowl (avoid aluminum or plastic).
2. Add the cream and whip quickly with a wire whisk until slightly thickened.
3. Add the sugar and vanilla extract and continue whipping until the cream forms soft peaks. Don't whip beyond soft peaks, or the cream will get stiff and curdled.
4. Cover and refrigerate until ready to use. (Keeps up to 24 hours.)
