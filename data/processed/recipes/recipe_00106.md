# Cherry & White-Chocolate Mousse Shortcakes

Enough for 8 biscuits. Mellow white chocolate and tart, juicy cherries contrast deliciously. Because the mousse is rich and creamy, there's no need to serve these shortcakes with whipped cream.

## Ingredients

- 8 Basic Shortcake Biscuits ***
- 2 cups heavy cream
- 8 oz. white chocolate, finely chopped
- 12 oz. (2 cups) pitted fresh cherries, soaked in 1/4 cup dark rum, and sprinkled with 1/4 cup sugar

*** = component recipe

## Directions

1. Start the mousse 8 to 24 hours before serving: bring 2/3 cup of the heavy cream to a boil, remove from the heat, and add the white chocolate. Stir until completely melted, then transfer to a medium bowl and let cool until tepid.
2. Put the remaining cream in a chilled bowl and whip until soft peaks form. With a rubber spatula, gently but quickly fold the whipped cream into the white chocolate mixture until just combined. Cover and refrigerate at least 8 hours.
3. Split the biscuits and place the bottoms on serving plates. Spoon the cherries and their juice onto the biscuits, then pipe the mousse around the top and bottom of each cake. Add the biscuit tops, spoon on more cherries and juice, and garnish with whole cherries.
