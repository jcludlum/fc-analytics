# Orange & Grand Marinier Shortcakes

Enough for 8 biscuits. Spike fresh oranges with a splash of Grand Marnier for a sophisticated shortcake. A few shavings of bitter chocolate add contrasting color and flavor.

## Ingredients

- 8 Basic Shortcake Biscuits ***
- Basic Whipped Cream ***
- 2 tsp. grated orange zest
- 5 oranges, peeled and sectioned, with their juice, soaked in 1/3 cup Grand Marnier liqueur, and sprinkled with 1/4 cup light brown sugar
- Grated bitter chocolate

*** = component recipe

## Directions

1. Add the orange zest to the Basic Shortcake Biscuits dough before baking.
2. Split the biscuits and place the bottoms on serving plates. Spoon the orange mixture and juice onto the biscuits and top with a small amount of the whipped cream. Add the biscuit tops, spoon on more orange and juice, add another dollop of whipped cream, and sprinkle with the bitter chocolate.
