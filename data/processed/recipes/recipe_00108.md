# Pineapple, Caramel & Roasted Macadamia Nut Shortcakes

Enough for 8 biscuits. Shortcake goes Hawaiian with fresh pineapple and roasted macadamia nuts, all bathed in sweet, amber caramel.

## Ingredients

- 8 Basic Shortcake Biscuits ***
- Basic Whipped Cream ***
- 2 1/4 cups sugar
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 2/3 cup macadamia nuts, salted or unsalted
- 1/2 beaten egg white
- 1 tsp. sugar
- 1 large fresh pineapple, peeled, cored, cut in 1/2-in. pieces, and sprinkled with 1/4 cup sugar

*** = component recipe

## Directions

1. Make the caramel sauce: put the 2 1/4 cups sugar in a large, heavy pan and heat, stirring constantly with a wooden spoon, until melted (there will be lumps); keep stirring until the lumps disappear and the sugar is a medium-dark amber.
2. Remove from the heat and carefully stir in 1/4 cup of the heavy cream (it will spatter). Add the rest of the cream and stir until completely smooth. Keep warm until ready to serve.
3. Glaze and roast the macadamia nuts: stir the nuts, egg white, and 1 tsp. sugar until combined. Spread on a parchment- or foil-lined baking sheet and bake 13 to 15 min. in a 325°F oven. Cool completely.
4. Split the biscuits and place the bottoms on serving plates. Spoon the pineapple and its juice onto the biscuits and top with a small amount of the whipped cream. Add the biscuit tops, spoon on more pineapple, then top with the caramel sauce and macadamia nuts.
